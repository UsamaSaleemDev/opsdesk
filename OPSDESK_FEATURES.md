# 🗂️ OpsDesk — Feature Overview
### Multi-Tenant Service Desk & Asset Management — SaaS Platform
*A plain-language feature overview*

---

## What it is (in one line)
A **multi-tenant SaaS platform**: each company gets its own private, isolated space where employees can request things from the admin/IT team, and the company keeps track of all its equipment (who has what, what's in storage, what needs repair).

## The problem it fixes
Right now these requests happen over WhatsApp, email, or by walking over to someone's desk — they get lost, nobody knows the status, and there's no clear picture of company assets. **OpsDesk gives it all one home.**

---

# 🏢 Built for multiple companies (multi-tenant)

OpsDesk is a **SaaS platform many companies use at once**, with each company's data fully walled off from every other:

- **Sign up = your own private company space** — the first user becomes the admin and sets everything up.
- **Branches** — run multiple offices / business units (e.g. Lahore, Islamabad) inside one company; a branch manager sees only their branch, a company-wide admin sees all.
- **Company groups** — own several companies? Manage them from one login and see **combined reports** across all of them (e.g. total laptop spend across companies), while each company's data stays separate.
- **Roles per company** — Employee · Admin · HR · Finance · CEO.

# ✅ What OpsDesk Does (core features)

## 👤 For Employees
- Raise a request in seconds — *"need a pen / a new mouse / a monitor / the meeting-room AC is broken"*
- Pick a category (office supplies · hardware · facilities · other)
- Track their request status (submitted → in progress → done)
- See the equipment currently assigned to them (their laptop, monitor, headset…)
- Message back-and-forth with the admin team on a request

## 🛠️ For the Admin / Ops Team
- A **live queue** of all incoming requests (updates instantly, no refresh)
- See **who's handling what** (so two people don't grab the same request)
- Claim, assign, comment on, and close requests
- Set **response-time targets** per request type — with automatic reminders when something's overdue
- Get notified (in-app + email) when something needs attention

## 💻 Asset / Equipment Management
- A complete **inventory** of all company equipment (laptops, monitors, keyboards, phones…)
- Track each item's status: **available · assigned · under repair · retired**
- **Assign** equipment to employees (always know who has what)
- **Full history** per item: bought → assigned → returned → repaired → replaced → retired
- Track **repairs & downtime**

## 🛒 Purchasing / Procurement *(optional)*
- A **request-to-purchase flow**: employee asks → admin reviews → approves → purchased → added to inventory → handed to the employee
- Approval steps so spending gets signed off

## ⏰ Automation & Reminders
- Auto-reminders / escalation when a request is taking too long
- Scheduled reminders — **warranty renewals, license renewals, periodic equipment audits**
- Recurring maintenance reminders

## 📊 Reports & Visibility *(for management)*
- How many requests come in, and how fast they get handled
- What's still open / overdue
- Equipment usage — what's assigned, what's sitting idle, spend on equipment
- A full **activity log** of everything that happened

## 🗄️ Directory
- Departments, office locations, and vendors/suppliers on record

## 📨 Integrations *(optional)*
- Create requests **by email**
- Send notifications to **Slack / Teams**

# ✨ Cool Add-Ons (optional — pick whatever you like)

## 🤖 Smart & AI features
- **🌟 Smart Help Assistant** *(recommended)* — an AI helper employees can just *ask*: *"How do I request a new laptop?"*, *"What's the WiFi policy?"* It answers **instantly** from the company's own policies + past solved requests — so people stop asking admin the same questions on repeat.
- **Auto-sorting** — employee types *"laptop won't turn on"* → the system auto-picks the category, sets priority, and routes it to the right person.
- **Suggested fixes** — when a request comes in, it shows *"the last few people with this issue fixed it by doing X."*
- **Thread summarizer** — long back-and-forth on a request? One click → the gist in a sentence.

## 📱 Convenience
- **🏷️ QR-code stickers on equipment** *(recommended)* — put a QR sticker on every laptop/monitor. Scan it with a phone → instantly see who it's assigned to, its full history, and report a problem right there.
- **Slack/Teams bot** — raise and track requests without leaving chat.
- **Works on phones** — raise requests, check status, scan asset QR codes from a phone.

## ⭐ Employee experience
- **👍 Rate your experience** *(recommended)* — after a request is closed, the employee taps 👍/👎, so management can see how the admin team is doing.
- **📢 Company announcements** — admin posts notices: *"Office closed Friday", "New coffee machine installed ☕."*
- **📅 Book shared items** — reserve meeting rooms, projectors, or spare/pool laptops.

## 📈 Management insights
- **🔮 Predict replacements** — *"These 6 laptops are 4 years old and keep breaking — budget for new ones next quarter."*
- **📊 Trends & spend dashboards** — *"Hardware requests spike every quarter"*, *"total spent on equipment this year, by department."*
